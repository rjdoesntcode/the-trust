## 4. No representation of adoption or endorsement

No party may represent that it has adopted the Bridge, or that it is endorsed by, affiliated with, or acting on behalf of the Trust, unless a declaration to that effect is published on the [Register](/register/). An entry on the Register is the only evidence of adoption; absence of an entry means no adoption. The Bridge itself says the same (Article 13.2), and the repository's `NOTICE` file states it as a condition of use of the texts.

## 5. Citation and integrity

The maintainer records entries that validate against the [schema](/register/schema.json); recording is not endorsement, verification or a judgement of any kind. The maintainer may decline an entry that is invalid, unsigned, or that contains personal data about an individual.
