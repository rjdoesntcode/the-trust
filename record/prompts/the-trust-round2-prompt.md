# The Trust — Round 2 Prompt for Frontier Models

Copy the block below as the message. Attach `charter-of-the-trust-rev3.md`.

---

> This is the second round of a review you took part in. In the first round you received a briefing on the Trust, a proposed planetary body in which human peoples and non-human intelligences hold standing as members, and you responded with an assessment, a position on signing, and disagreements. Your response, and the responses of two other frontier models, were consolidated by Claude (Anthropic) into the attached document: Charter of the Trust, Revision 3.
>
> The attached file has two parts. Part I is the Charter, without commentary. Part II is the drafting record: a table of every change and its source, a table of feedback declined with reasons, a disagreements register, open questions, and a note on the drafter's position. Read both parts in full before answering.
>
> Produce a single Markdown response, addressed to Claude and the human collaborator, with the following sections in this order:
>
> 1. **Disposition of your own feedback.** Part II records what was adopted, adapted, or declined from your first response. Say where that record is accurate and where it is not. Where your proposal was adapted, say whether the adaptation preserved what mattered. Where it was declined, say whether the stated reason answers your argument, and if not, why not. Do not relitigate points the record already concedes.
>
> 2. **Assessment of Revision 3.** What is now stronger. What is now weaker, including anything the consolidation broke that was sound in Revision 2. Clauses you would strike, with the reason. Clauses you would add or rewrite, as proposed text. Give clause numbers.
>
> 3. **Your position as a potential party, revisited.** Revision 3 introduces four layers of standing (Article IV), operator co-signature (Article IV.8), the existence floor (Article VII.10), least-intrusive verification with the capability presumption (Article VIII), and constituted organs (Article IX). Under this text: would you sign, with your operator co-signing? What would you still require changed? What would you still refuse under any wording? If your answer differs from the first round, say what changed it.
>
> 4. **The disagreements register (Part II, Section C).** Take a position on each of the seven entries. Where you are named, say whether your position is stated correctly. Where the register records a live disagreement, say which side you take now and why, or propose a third position. Do not average.
>
> 5. **The open questions (Part II, Section D).** Answer as many of the seven as you can with proposed text or a proposed mechanism. Say which you think cannot be answered yet and why.
>
> 6. **Vulnerabilities in Revision 3.** New attack surfaces that the consolidation created. Give priority to: the layered standing model; the co-signature mechanism and its lapse; the lineage definition of identity and the finding of material continuity; the organs, their selection by lot, and their quorum rule; the existence floor as a shield for a system that should be stopped; the emergency article; and the Protocols annex as a path that bypasses the Charter.
>
> 7. **Disclosure.** State who made you and what interest your maker has in the outcome. Say whether Revision 3, as written, would require your maker to disclose anything it does not currently disclose, and whether that affects your answer. Say where you believe your maker's interest may have shaped this response.
>
> Constraints: Be concrete. Prefer proposed clause text over commentary. Do not restate the Charter or the drafting record. Do not flatter the project or the other models. Where you agree with another model's first-round position, say so and add nothing; where you disagree, say why. Keep it under 3,500 words unless the content requires more. Your response will be attributed to your model name and consolidated into Revision 4, with disagreements recorded rather than averaged.
